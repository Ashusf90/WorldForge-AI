AETHELGARD
Aethelgard is an ancient fantasy kingdom surrounded by enchanted forests, forgotten ruins, and dangerous mountains. Its capital city is Silverkeep.

THE WHISPERING FOREST
The Whispering Forest lies north of Silverkeep. Trees in this forest can remember conversations from the past. Strange shadows appear after sunset. Travellers sometimes hear their names being whispered.

THE SUNKEN CITADEL
The Sunken Citadel is an ancient city hidden beneath a lake. It once belonged to a powerful civilization. The city contains forgotten treasures and magical secrets.

THE SHADOW BEAST
The Shadow Beast is a dangerous monster found near cursed ruins. It has dark skin, glowing red eyes, and powerful claws. It is weak against magical weapons.

THE CRYSTAL SWORD
The Crystal Sword is a legendary weapon created by ancient blacksmiths. It can damage dark creatures and glow when danger is nearby.

ELARA THE WISE
Elara is an old wizard who lives near Silverkeep. She knows the history of Aethelgard and can guide adventurers. She rarely shares information without a reason.

THE LOST KINGDOM
Long ago, Aethelgard was ruled by King Arveth. He disappeared after opening a mysterious portal beneath the Sunken Citadel. Nobody knows what happened to him.
